# Yushan Xu 徐羽杉
**PhD Student, Media Arts and Sciences**  
Indiana University Bloomington  
Graduate Assistant, Kinetic Immersion and Extended Reality (KIX) Lab

I am a PhD student in Media Arts and Sciences at **Indiana University Bloomington**, where I work in the **KIX Lab**.  
My research focuses on **immersive media and spatial audio**, and on how people perceive and experience sound in virtual and mixed environments.

Before IU, I worked as a research assistant at the Audio Experience Design Lab, Imperial College London, on **HRTFs and the precedence effect**.  
My approach combines **audio engineering, perceptual experimentation, interactive visualization, and tangible interface design**.

My practice also bridges the **tangible and the invisible**, through speculative and multisensory projects such as *Mushroom* and *Smell the Sound*.  
These works explore how **perception, emotion, and interaction** shape spatial experience, embodiment, and connection.

This website presents my **selected design works**.  
Feel free to explore my [Portfolio](/portfolio/).
